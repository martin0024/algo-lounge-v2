Calculate the total cost for coffee shop orders including 15% tax, rounded to the nearest dollar.

**Menu Prices:**

- Espresso: $3
- Latte: $5
- Croissant: $6

## Example

**Example 1**

```
Input:  ["Espresso", "Latte", "Croissant"]
Output: 16
```

($3 + $5 + $6) × 1.15 = $16.10 → $16

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ number of items ≤ 100
- Items can repeat
- Round to nearest dollar after tax
