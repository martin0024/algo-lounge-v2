You ordered a box online. What arrived was this. The return policy is “all sales final.” The seller’s username was “definitely\_a\_box.” Determine if they were right. The shape is a connected arrangement of square panels, represented by an `n × n` binary grid with `1` as a panel and `0` as empty space. Determine whether the panels form a valid net for **any** rectangular box with positive integer dimensions *a × b × c*; if so, return `[a, b, c]` sorted in ascending order, otherwise return an empty array.

A valid fold must satisfy all of the following:

- Every panel is used **exactly once**
- No two panels overlap after folding
- Every face of the resulting box is completely covered
- All panels must form a **connected region** — a disconnected region cannot be folded into a single box
